# Privacy policy: writing-editor

Last updated: 2026-11-24

writing-editor is an open-source plugin of instructions for Claude. The author runs no server and no service for it, and receives, collects, stores and shares no data from anyone who uses it.

It reads only the draft you give it, inside your Claude session.

Anything you send to Claude is handled under Anthropic's own terms and privacy policy, not by this plugin's author.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/dougkeiller/writing-editor/issues.
